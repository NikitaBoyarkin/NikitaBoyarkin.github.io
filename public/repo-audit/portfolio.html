4. How to present

  • Headline number: lead with "+5.72pp KYC conversion (p<0.0001) → €656K/yr at 44× ROI". It is the only figure with significance, a business unit, and an ROI in one line — and it is the one the profile README and the portfolio site already promote.
  • Feature 3–5, in this order: (1) volta-banking as the umbrella; (2) case-ab (the randomized proof behind the headline); (3) case-causal-kyc (DiD +9.09pp on N=84,000 — shows you don't confuse correlation with causation); (4) case-retention (M1 +11.8pp, +€227K/yr — shows the effect survived in cohorts); (5) serp-rank-lab (+38% revenue/search) or sql-analytics-case-study (funnel −54%) for a second domain.
  • Second headline for method depth: "CUPED removes 26% of variance — same power on 5.5k users/arm instead of 10k" (causal-uplift) and "18 A/B modules, each calibration re-run as a test" (ab_test). This is what separates a methods portfolio from a charts portfolio.
  • Show decision-grade judgment, not just wins. Three READMEs state negative results as results: case-dormant-winback (human ROI 0.40 → kill), case-assisted-cac (45+ LTV/CAC 0.66 → hold), case-anchor-cac (LTV/CAC 1.76 at SOM → hold). Lead a bullet with "killed a channel and held a launch on the evidence" — it is more credible than another positive lift.
  • Quantify breadth with one line: "23 linked case studies under one neobank flagship; 17 case studies on the live bilingual portfolio site."
  • For a BI/analytics role, swap the top tooling pick to sql-analytics-case-study (25 DuckDB cases + dbt layer + a real-data case) and churn-prediction (leakage-free split, lift@top-decile 5.44×).
  • Be explicit that the Volta numbers are synthetic (seeded generators) and point to the one real-data artifact: SQL case 26 on UCI Online Retail II (1,067,371 invoice lines, 72.4% repeat). Honesty here protects the headline.
  • Drop from the resume/landing entirely: NLTK, Python_algorithms, Python_data_structures, TensorFlow-and-Keras, my_cv, telegram_bot_public — no quantified result and they dilute the analyst signal. Keep them only as a one-line "archive" link if a breadth signal is needed.
  • Do not headline the infra repo counts. "10 games", "2 DAGs", "64 tests", "34 tests", "≥80% coverage" are engineering metadata, not outcomes. Mention tooling as capability (Airflow, dbt, DuckDB, Streamlit, Postgres, Supabase) rather than as results.
  • If one number must carry a landing hero, use +5.72pp / €656K/yr; if two, add +38% revenue per search (serp-rank-lab) to show the same rigor outside the flagship's domain.
