Onboarding funnel

Where do new users drop off between install and first transaction?

KYC completion is the critical step: the largest relative drop in the funnel. Registration loses the most users in absolute terms — two lenses, reported both.

KYC progress-bar A/B test

Does a progress bar in the KYC flow lift completion?

Treatment lifts completion by +5.72pp (p<0.0001, no SRM) — ship. This is the randomized claim the causal layer later strengthens.

Cohort retention

Did the fix hold up in long-term retention?

Post-fix cohorts retain better from M1 onward (+9.2pp at M3). The gap persists across the curve, not just at one month.

Causal layer — difference-in-differences

Was the retention lift caused by the fix, or just a shared time trend?

DiD ATT = +9.09pp: the treated flow rises at the cutoff while the comparison flow does not. Pre-trends flat, placebo null, covariates balanced.

Customer segmentation

Who are the users and where does revenue concentrate?

The top segment is 12% of users but ~41% of revenue — a classic concentration story that drives the per-segment monetization strategy.

Market & Jobs — premium upsell

Does the premium upsell transfer to every job segment?

The anchor segment converts at ~17% while Digital Newcomers 45+ sit near 2% — the value proposition does not transfer. Segment-specific offers, not one upsell.

Market & Jobs — referral conversion

Does referral scale to new segments?

Referral converts best in the anchor and collapses for 45+ and family budgeters — don't scale referral spend before segment-specific incentives.

Market & Jobs — segment sizes

How big is each job segment in the simulated base?

Five JTBD segments simulated at scale — the population the Market & Jobs layer reasons about.
